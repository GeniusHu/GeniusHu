## Hi, I'm Hu 👋

I build free browser-based tools for education and crafts.

### Projects

- **[Looprel](https://looprel.com)** — Photo to crochet chart, cross stitch pattern & embroidery QR code generator. Free, no sign-up, runs in your browser. ([repo](https://github.com/GeniusHu/looprel))
- **[CursiveAZ](https://cursiveaz.com)** — Free printable cursive alphabet worksheets A-Z, with stroke-order diagrams and teaching guides. ([repo](https://github.com/GeniusHu/cursiveaz))
- **[Verbatimly](https://verbatimly.ai)** — Transcribe video to text in the browser. The video is never uploaded — audio is extracted locally, transcript comes back with timestamps, speaker labels, and SRT/VTT export. ([repo](https://github.com/GeniusHu/verbatimly))

### Other experiments

- **[SpellGenie](https://getspellgenie.com)** — Spelling test tool for grades 1-8. Paste a word list, kids hear each word and type it. ([repo](https://github.com/GeniusHu/spellgenie))
- **[MathMill](https://getmathmill.com)** — Math worksheet generator for teachers, parents and homeschoolers. ([repo](https://github.com/GeniusHu/mathmill))
- **[MeshAvatarStudio](https://meshavatarstudio.com)** — Mesh gradient avatar maker. ([repo](https://github.com/GeniusHu/meshavatarstudio))

### What I care about

- **Zero-friction tools** — no sign-up, no upload, everything runs client-side
- **Real utility over hype** — tools that solve a specific problem for a specific audience
- **SEO-driven product development** — build what people are actually searching for
